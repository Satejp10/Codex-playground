How DeepIntent makes money

DeepIntent sells a healthcare-specific demand-side platform and campaign services to pharmaceutical brands and agencies. The economic engine is media activation plus data, optimization, measurement, and custom support around HCP, DTC, and integrated campaigns.

Why it is differentiated

  • Purpose-built for healthcare, not a generalist DSP.
  • Connects media, identity, and real-world clinical data.
  • Optimizes toward pharma KPIs: audience quality, script lift, adherence, and verified patient reach.
  • Omnichannel activation: CTV, audio, video, display/native, DOOH, and EHR.
